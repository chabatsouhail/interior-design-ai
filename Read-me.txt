APARTMENT STUDIO

Extract the entire ZIP, then double-click Start-Apartment-Studio.cmd.
The included My-Apartment.glb is a separate model you can open in Blender.
Keep the launcher window open while you use the apartment. The launcher
opens your browser on this computer. It uses Node.js 22 or later.

Click Walkthrough to start in the hallway. Use WASD to walk and the
mouse to look. Esc frees the cursor. T opens and focuses Design chat.
If mouse capture is unavailable, drag to look and use WASD to move.
Rooms lets you enter a particular room directly.

Click once to pin the first visible object. Hold Shift to see the live
segment and Shift-click each additional corner. Walk between clicks
if you need a better angle. On a flat surface, three or more ordered
corners show the filled area. Backspace removes the last point.

In Design chat, choose Continue with ChatGPT, select your account and
allow plan usage. On return, the app restores your pins and draft.
Examples: select the plastic chair and ask for a sage green finish.
Outline a wall and ask for a cat wall maze with staggered platforms.
The assistant can recolor model objects, build objects from groups of
geometric parts in a selected area, remove additions, and answer questions.
Submit only when you are ready. Your request, selected geometry, scene
inventory and recent chat context go to OpenAI. The source video stays
outside the app.

Edits are saved in this browser at the local app address. Use Undo and
Redo at the top. Model settings lets you adjust the estimated dimensions
and export the current full-height apartment to a .glb file for Blender.
Closing the launcher stops the local service. Reopen it to continue.

The plan follows your sketch. The walkthrough footage supplies the
visible finishes and furnishings. The exterior view, room dimensions,
fixture positions and concealed surfaces are approximations. The
unfilmed second bedroom is an unfurnished room shell.
